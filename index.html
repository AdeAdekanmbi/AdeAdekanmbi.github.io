Who I Am

I am an engineer and researcher with roots in Nigeria, passionate about creating technologies that support people and the world. My journey has taken me from rebuilding homes in Puerto Rico to doing research at MIT, always guided by service, curiosity, and perseverance.

At Howard University, I've found a community that sharpens both my technical skills and my sense of purpose. I care deeply about accessibility and applying my experience in machine learning and hardware engineering to design systems that make everyday life safer and more intuitive. I hope to use my work to expand opportunities for others in the same way many people invested in me.

Beyond the lab, I enjoy mentoring students and building community, hanging out with friends and serving others, playing piano, and playing football. These keep me grounded and energized for the journey ahead.
